DRONE

This was my first major hands-on engineering project, built with my brother's help. It introduced me to the practical challenges of making something fly: electronics, balance, component integration, and flight control.

About the build

My first major engineering endeavor, developed with my brother, provided deep hands-on experience navigating the real-world challenges of aerodynamics, electronics, component integration, and system control

What I learned

  • Basic flight mechanics and stability
  • How motors, propellers, and power systems work together
  • The importance of balance and weight distribution
  • Practical troubleshooting during hardware assembly

Parts & links

Frame

Main chassis that holds all components together and sets motor spacing and overall weight.

  • Model: F450 quadcopter kit
  • Wheelbase: 450 mm
Frame product page →

Motors

Brushless motors that spin the propellers and produce thrust.

  • Model: Ready to Sky RS2212
  • KV: 920 KV
  • Type: Brushless outrunner
  • Count: 4
CW & CCW Motor product page →

Electronic Speed Controllers (ESCs)

Control motor speed based on signals from the flight controller.

  • Rating: 30 A each
  • Firmware: SimonK
  • Count: 4 (with connectors)
ESC product page →

Flight Controller

The brain of the drone. It reads sensor data and pilot inputs and keeps the drone stable.

  • Board: ArduPilot APM 2.8
  • Firmware: ArduPilot (ArduCopter)
  • Onboard gyro/accelerometer and barometer
Flight controller page →

Battery

LiPo battery that powers the entire system. Cell count and capacity set the voltage and flight time.

  • Brand: Orange
  • Cells: 3S (7.4 V nominal)
Battery product page →

Radio system (TX & RX)

Handheld transmitter and onboard receiver for pilot control.

  • Type: FlySky 6-channel, 2.4 GHz
  • Receiver: FS-R6B
  • Channels: 6
