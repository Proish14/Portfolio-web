News coverage Akola · August 15, 2026

Akola: Over 1,500 Free Saplings Distributed on Independence Day 2026

On the occasion of India's 80th Independence Day, more than 1,500 free saplings were distributed in Akola through the joint efforts of My Vasudha Vikas Sanstha, Shri Sankalp Mitra Parivar, and Nisargkriti. The initiative aimed to promote environmental awareness and encourage citizens to contribute towards a greener future.

The drive highlighted the importance of tree plantation and the role of trees in improving air quality, supporting biodiversity, and maintaining ecological balance. By distributing saplings free of cost, the participating organizations encouraged citizens to take small but meaningful steps towards nature conservation.

The collaboration reflected a shared commitment to community welfare. Celebrating Independence Day through this initiative conveyed a meaningful message: serving the nation also involves protecting its natural resources and working towards a healthier future for generations to come.
